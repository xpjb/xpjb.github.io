# Real People on the Internet
I'm sure when Tim Berners-Lee invented the web he didn't anticipate that we would all be stuck doomscrolling reels on our phones. Judging by the choice of the letter 'a', you can tell that hyperlinks were supposed to be one of the principal components of the system that would foster the exploration of great unknown frontiers. In celebration of that, I have created this page, a collection of links to other (seemingly, if its not all one big AI generated simulation) real people on the internet. 

The Topics I highlight may not be completely representative, but specific to what I engaged with in particular.
